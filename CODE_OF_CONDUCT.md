# Code of conduct

Helpercraft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text is at that link. In short:

- Be kind and welcoming, whatever someone's background, experience or way of speaking.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or sharing anyone's private information.
- These rules apply in this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting a problem

Email **cloverseq@gmail.com**. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept private. You'll get a reply, and the steps taken follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
